Good To Know

How To Play

A few simple points for students and parents.

▶

Press Play

Open a game and press the green play button to begin. Use the stop button to end the game and the arrows button to play in full screen.

⌨

Use The Keyboard

Most games are played with the arrow keys or the space bar. The keys for each game are listed on its card above.

📶

Works Offline

Each game is a single page that loads completely, so it keeps working even if the internet connection is slow.

🏫

Made By Students

These projects come from our computer classes. New games will be added here as students complete them.
